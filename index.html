How to play

Pair to 14

Cards are worth their rank (A=1 … K=13). Select two uncovered cards totaling 14 to remove them. A card is uncovered when both cards below it are gone (the bottom row is always uncovered).

Stock and waste

Press D or Draw to flip a stock card to the waste. The top waste card can pair with any uncovered pyramid card.

Recycle

When the stock is empty, press R to flip the waste back into a new stock — a limited number of times.

Keyboard

Arrows move between legal cards · Enter select · Esc cancel/pause · U undo · H hint · D draw · R recycle · C reset camera.

Scoring

+100 per pair · +1000 clearing the pyramid · +10 per unused stock card · +100 per unused recycle · −25 per invalid action.
